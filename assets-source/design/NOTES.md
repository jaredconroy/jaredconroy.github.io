Consumer Think Tank - A website which I was brought in to design and develop the front-end for. The idea was for users to go on there and give product ideas to companies and upvote ideas that they thought should get made, with the idea being that ideas could create buzz and catch the eye of the company or replace the need for a corporate thinktank and instead put that into the hands of the consumer. In the CTT folder, favicon.png is the app icon, logo.png is the full logo and wordmark, and ui_mockup.png is a mockup of my UI design for the website. Perhaps I will port a small demo of the site to the point we developed it at some point, but the project was ditched years ago.
