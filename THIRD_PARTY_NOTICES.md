Synthetic test audio is retained as fixtures and is not in the active playlist.

The Sleds band photograph was supplied by Jared Conroy. YouTube video thumbnails and official video players remain remotely hosted by YouTube; The Sleds artist player remains remotely hosted by Spotify. No audio or video from those services is extracted or redistributed in this repository.
